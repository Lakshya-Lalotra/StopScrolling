# Changelog

Each version is published under [Releases](../../releases) with its APK.

## 4.1
- **Instagram removes ads and suggested posts itself.** When one appears in the Instagram app, ScrollStop opens its ⋯ menu and taps "Not interested" or "Hide ad", the way you would, behind a plain screen so you don't see the menu. At most 8 a minute.
- **Facebook:** sponsored and suggested posts are removed by tapping the post's own ✕ / Hide button.
- A reel a friend sends you in DMs stays allowed until you swipe to someone else's reel.
- More Reels / Shorts / Spotlight screens are recognised.
- Ads labelled just "Ad" (Instagram's newer label) are now caught, and so are posts with an "Order now" / "Shop now" style button bar.
- Fixed the whole feed being covered on Instagram versions that wrap all posts in one container: only the ad or suggested post is hidden.
- Hidden posts are covered without any text, and ScrollStop scrolls past them sooner. If Android hasn't granted the swipe permission yet, it uses Instagram's own scrolling instead.

## 4.0
- Simpler screens: Home, Apps (one row per app, tap it for its switches), Stats and Settings.
- Suggested posts and ads are covered inside the Instagram app's own feed, and ScrollStop scrolls past them.
- Help → "Something isn't blocked?" saves a screen's layout so detection can be tuned to your Instagram version.

## 3.2
- First version that hides suggested posts and ads inside the real Instagram app.
- Settings panel (⚙) inside ScrollStop's Instagram; searching for people still works when Explore is blocked.
- Guide and 30-day reminder for Instagram's own "Snooze suggested posts" switch.

## 3.1
- ScrollStop's Instagram removes ads, suggested posts, posts from accounts you don't follow, and everything after "You're all caught up".
- Option to open ScrollStop's Instagram whenever the Instagram app is opened.

## 3.0
- Facebook, X, Reddit and LinkedIn inside ScrollStop, next to Instagram and YouTube, with the feeds taken out.
- Share a link to ScrollStop to watch just that one reel or Short.
- Instagram Following / Favorites feed; Explore and Popular pages blocked on YouTube, X and Reddit.
- Optional adult-content filter, Sleep mode on chosen nights with a heads-up, "locked in" stat, home-screen widget.

## 2.2
- Instagram and YouTube inside ScrollStop with the Reels, Explore and Shorts buttons removed.
- Sleep mode: social apps close at bedtime.

## 2.1
- The cover over the Reels / Shorts button takes the app's own colour instead of showing a black box.

## 2.0
- Rebuilt blocker: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight, in the apps and in browsers.
- Daily allowance by time or number of videos, limits for any app, strict mode (timer, PIN or sentence), blocking hours, breaks and stats.
- Every build is signed with the same key, so new versions install over old ones.
